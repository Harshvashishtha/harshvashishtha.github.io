<div class="res-group">
<h2 class="section-title">Databases &amp; tools</h2>
<ul class="res-list">
  <li><a href="http://flweb.janelia.org/cgi-bin/flew.cgi" rel="noopener">Janelia FlyLight</a>
    <p>Transgenic <em>Drosophila</em> lines with characterised neuronal expression patterns, for visualising and manipulating specific cell types.</p></li>
  <li><a href="https://neuprint.janelia.org/" rel="noopener">Janelia neuPrint</a>
    <p>Explore, query and share connectome data from <em>Drosophila</em> brains.</p></li>
  <li><a href="https://flybase.org/" rel="noopener">FlyBase</a>
    <p>The reference database for <em>Drosophila</em> genes, genomes and genetic data, with annotation and analysis tools.</p></li>
  <li><a href="https://bdsc.indiana.edu/" rel="noopener">Bloomington Drosophila Stock Center</a>
    <p>Stock centre for mutants, transgenics and balancers.</p></li>
  <li><a href="https://www.virtualflybrain.org/" rel="noopener">Virtual Fly Brain</a>
    <p>Anatomy, gene expression and neuron morphology for the fly brain, in one browsable atlas.</p></li>
  <li><a href="https://www.neuinfo.org/" rel="noopener">Neuroscience Information Framework</a>
    <p>Cross-cutting index of neuroscience tools, data and literature, including fly-specific resources.</p></li>
</ul>
</div>
